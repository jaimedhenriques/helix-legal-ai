## Claude legal/source insights incorporated

- Legal teams are using AI for contract review, drafting, redlining, extraction, comparison, and day-to-day document work.
- First-pass review is the key wedge: AI handles routine review so lawyer time goes to judgment.
- Legal summarization should use explicit extraction fields and consistent structure.
- Missing facts should be labeled “Not specified.”
- Summary quality requires defined success criteria and systematic evaluation.
- Legal AI carries liability risk; outputs should be reviewed by legal professionals.
- Real deployments must address PDF/DOCX/text ingestion, long-document strategy, security, privacy, rollout, commercial credentials, and model/vendor terms.

## Recommended submission narrative

Helix Legal AI is a workflow-first legal AI product for lean legal teams. It is not positioned as autonomous legal advice. The MVP narrows to first-pass contract review and intake triage, uses customer-approved playbooks, produces structured and source-grounded outputs, and requires human legal approval before export.
