Helix Legal AI — GTM Plan

Strategic thesis

The near-term opportunity is not broad “AI for law.” It is a focused workflow wedge for lean legal teams that need immediate relief from contract review, intake triage, and document summarization without a major CLM migration.

Positioning

Primary message

“First-pass contract review and legal intake, governed by your playbook and approved by your lawyers.”

Supporting messages

  • Reduce repetitive document review without losing control.
  • Convert legal playbooks into operational workflows.
  • Give business teams faster answers while preserving lawyer approval.
  • Keep every AI output traceable, editable, and auditable.

Demo script

Demo setup

Persona: commercial counsel at 400-person B2B SaaS company.

Document: third-party NDA.

Goal: identify non-standard terms and produce a negotiation memo.

Script

1. Open with pain

- “This is for legal teams that do not want another chatbot. They want a governed first-pass review workflow.”

2. Create matter

- Show matter intake fields: counterparty, agreement type, urgency, business owner, governing playbook.

3. Upload document

- Upload NDA sample.

- Explain parsing and source-grounded analysis.

4. Structured summary

- Show parties, effective date, term, confidentiality period, residuals, return/destruction, governing law, assignment, remedies.

- Point out “not specified” fields.

5. Playbook comparison

- Show flagged issues:

- Non-mutual confidentiality obligations.

- Overbroad residuals clause.

- Injunctive relief language outside policy.

- Missing data protection clause.

- Show severity and rationale.

6. Suggested fallback language

- Show redline-style suggestions.

- Emphasize configurable customer playbook language.

7. Human approval

- Reviewer accepts two findings, edits one, rejects one.

- Show audit entry.

8. Export

- Export legal memo for sales and clause notes for negotiation.

9. Close

- “The goal is not to replace counsel. The goal is to move counsel from first-pass extraction to judgment.”

Objection handling

“We already have a CLM.”

Helix can start where CLM adoption is weak: first-pass analysis, playbook enforcement, and legal intake. It can export into existing systems rather than replace them.

“AI hallucination is too risky.”

Helix constrains outputs to structured templates, requires source snippets, marks missing info as “not specified,” and requires legal review before export.

“Security will block this.”

Start with sanitized docs or low-risk agreement types, provide DPA/security pack, define retention/deletion, and restrict pilot users.

“Lawyers won’t trust it.”

Run shadow mode against historical documents and measure acceptance/rejection rates before live use.
